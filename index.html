Customer Support Agents from Scratch

I can build customer support agents that answer questions, understand intent, use company knowledge, create tickets, route urgent issues, escalate to humans, and work across web chat, email, WhatsApp, and helpdesk flows.

  • Knowledge-base connected support with company-specific rules.
  • Ticket creation, tagging, priority detection, and routing.
  • Customer history, conversation summaries, and human handoff.
  • Analytics for repeated complaints, resolution quality, and support performance.
02

AI Calling Agents from Scratch

I can build inbound and outbound calling agents that speak with customers, collect details, qualify leads, book appointments, follow up, summarize calls, and connect with CRM/helpdesk systems.

  • Speech-to-text, LLM reasoning, text-to-speech, and live call flow logic.
  • Appointment booking, order updates, lead qualification, and reminders.
  • Call transcript, summary, sentiment, action items, and escalation notes.
  • Safety rules for consent, sensitive data, and human transfer.
03

AI‑Integrated Shopping Websites

If a customer wants an AI shopping website, I can build an ecommerce platform where users search, ask, compare, and buy through an intelligent assistant integrated with products, cart, orders, and support.

  • Personalized recommendations, comparisons, bundles, size/color guidance, and budget-based suggestions.
  • Checkout guidance, cart recovery, order tracking, returns support, and after-sales help.
  • Admin automation for product descriptions, FAQs, inventory insights, and customer questions.
